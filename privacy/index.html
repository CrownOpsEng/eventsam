EventSam Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

CrownOpsEng operates EventSam. This Privacy Policy explains what information CrownOpsEng receives or stores when EventSam uses its external Action service, why that information is processed, how it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

Scope. This policy covers CrownOpsEng's processing through the EventSam Action service and the public EventSam site. OpenAI separately processes ChatGPT accounts, conversations, files, browsing activity, and Action requests under OpenAI's own terms and privacy policies.

1. Data we process

EventSam is designed to research events and maintain business research and planning records. Depending on how it is used, CrownOpsEng may process the following categories of information:

  • Information submitted for EventSam operations. Information that a user asks EventSam to save, retrieve, evaluate, organize, or otherwise use through the external Action service. This may include names, business contact information, event, venue, organizer or vendor details, notes, links, dates, requirements, planning information, and other content relevant to the user's request.
  • Research information. Facts, excerpts, source references, URLs, dates, and other information obtained from public or authorized third-party sources while researching events and related business opportunities.
  • Generated planning records. Assessments, opportunity decisions, concerns, follow-up work, monitoring items, lists, and other records created to carry out the requested research and planning workflow.
  • Operational provenance. The operator identity associated with an Action credential, operation type, request and processing timestamps, durable request identifiers, service results, and limited technical metadata needed to maintain reliable history, diagnose failures, and protect the service.
  • Authentication information. EventSam sends a private credential with Action requests. CrownOpsEng's application stores a one-way cryptographic hash of the credential rather than the plaintext credential. Infrastructure providers may transiently process request headers while delivering the request.
  • Infrastructure information. Hosting and network providers may process ordinary technical information such as IP addresses, request metadata, timestamps, and service logs as part of operating and securing their infrastructure.

2. How we use data

CrownOpsEng processes this information to:

  • provide the research, planning, recordkeeping, follow-up, and retrieval functions requested through EventSam;
  • maintain continuity and history across EventSam sessions;
  • reduce duplicate or conflicting records and preserve source provenance;
  • authenticate authorized operators and protect the service from unauthorized use;
  • diagnose errors, maintain reliability, and improve the operation of the service;
  • respond to privacy, security, or support requests; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users, CrownOpsEng, and others.

3. Service providers and third parties

CrownOpsEng uses third-party infrastructure to provide EventSam. In particular:

  • Supabase provides hosted database and API infrastructure used by the EventSam Action service. Supabase may process data as an infrastructure provider according to its own terms and privacy practices.
  • OpenAI provides ChatGPT and the Custom GPT environment through which EventSam is used. OpenAI determines its own processing of ChatGPT conversations, account information, browsing, files, and Action requests. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub hosts this public policy through GitHub Pages. This site does not intentionally use analytics or advertising scripts, but GitHub may process ordinary technical request information under its own policies.
  • External research sources have their own privacy practices. When EventSam researches public websites or other services through ChatGPT's available browsing capabilities, those interactions are subject to the relevant providers' terms and policies.

CrownOpsEng does not sell personal information and does not use EventSam data for targeted advertising.

4. When data may be disclosed

CrownOpsEng may disclose information to service providers that support EventSam, when a user directs or authorizes the disclosure, when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse or security incidents, or when required by law or a valid legal process. CrownOpsEng does not disclose EventSam records to advertisers for advertising purposes.

5. Retention

EventSam is intended to preserve useful research and planning history, so records may be retained while they remain useful for those purposes. CrownOpsEng may delete or de-identify information when it is no longer reasonably needed, in response to a valid deletion request, or as part of normal system maintenance.

Some information may be retained longer when reasonably necessary for security, fraud or abuse prevention, service integrity, backup recovery, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Revoked credential records and limited operational provenance may also be retained to preserve security and audit history.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, a person may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

Privacy requests can be sent to hello@crownopseng.com. Please provide enough information to identify the relevant EventSam records. CrownOpsEng may need to verify the requester's identity or authority before acting on a request.

Some requests may be limited where retention or processing is required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, service integrity, or the rights of others.

7. Security

CrownOpsEng uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service, including restricted Action credentials and one-way credential hashing. No method of transmission, hosting, or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Suspected security issues can be reported to security@crownopseng.com.

8. International processing

CrownOpsEng and its service providers may process information in Canada and other jurisdictions in which they operate. Privacy and data-protection laws may differ between jurisdictions. Where required, CrownOpsEng will handle information in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children

EventSam is not intended for children under 13, and CrownOpsEng does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13 through the Action service. If you believe such information has been submitted, contact CrownOpsEng so it can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this policy

CrownOpsEng may update this Privacy Policy as EventSam, its service providers, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes materially.
